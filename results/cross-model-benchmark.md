# Cross-model tokenizer benchmark (Polish)

Generated by `scripts/cross_model_benchmark.py` on FLORES-200 devtest pol_Latn (1012 sentences). Tokens/sentence for each model's **stock** tokenizer vs our Polish tokenizer applied by **extend** (graft, base ids kept) and **replace** (standalone). Reduction % = cost-savings % (cost is linear in tokens).

| Model | Stock tok/sent | Extend | Replace | Stock enc tok/s | Ours enc tok/s |
|-------|---------------:|--------|---------|----------------:|---------------:|
| gpt2 | 71.64 | 53.5 (−25.3%) | 30.12 (−58.0%) | 1,546,411 | 1,103,854 |
| llama | 50.76 | 36.01 (−29.1%) | 30.12 (−40.7%) | 517,070 | 1,103,854 |
| mistral | 56.32 | n/a | 30.12 (−46.5%) | 2,064,656 | 1,103,854 |
| qwen | 48.17 | 44.04 (−8.6%) | 30.12 (−37.5%) | 921,634 | 1,103,854 |
| deepseek | 45.0 | 40.89 (−9.1%) | 30.12 (−33.1%) | 979,117 | 1,103,854 |

Encode speed is CPU tokenizer throughput (not inference latency — that needs a served model on GPU, projected by `tokenizer_core.economics`). `replace` gives the largest token cut (full standalone efficiency) but needs an embedding transplant + short retrain; `extend` keeps English intact for a smaller Polish cut. Full data in `cross-model-benchmark.json`.
