The same recipe-driven pipeline handles every FLORES script (Latin, Cyrillic, Hebrew, Arabic, Devanagari, Thai, Han) - copy `languages/_template/`, stage a corpus with `scripts/stage_corpus.py`, and run. Each 32k BPE cuts tokens 23-68% vs baselines; most languages reach ~1.0-1.3x English (Hebrew/Chinese below parity). The very dense scripts - Hindi (Devanagari) and Thai - stay higher at this vocab/data budget and want a larger vocab (48-64k) and more corpus to close fully.
