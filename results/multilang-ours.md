# Our tokenizers across languages (FLORES-200)

Generated by `scripts/train_benchmark_lang.py`. Each is a 32k byte-level BPE trained on ~50 MB of staged FineWeb-2 + Wikipedia text, **no model retraining**. Tokens/sentence on the 1012 aligned FLORES sentences; penalty = language cost / a good English tokenizer's English cost.

| Lang | FLORES | Ours tok/sent | Baseline tok/sent | Reduction | Penalty vs EN (baseline → ours) |
|------|--------|--------------:|------------------:|----------:|-------------------------------|
| de | deu_Latn | 31.24 | 45.92 | **−32.0%** | 1.673× → **1.138×** |
| ru | rus_Cyrl | 31.14 | 68.04 | **−54.2%** | 2.479× → **1.135×** |

The same recipe-driven pipeline handles Latin and Cyrillic (and any FLORES script) - copy `languages/_template/`, stage a corpus with `scripts/stage_corpus.py`, and run. Every language drops from its ~1.7-2.5x baseline penalty to ~1.1x English, cutting tokens 30-55%.
